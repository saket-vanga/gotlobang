Quick fixes

A shop “didn't answer” or prices are missing

Shops' websites are sometimes slow or busy. Open the list again, or go to Settings › Check all prices again. If one shop keeps failing, you can switch it off in Settings for now — and tell us below so we can fix it.

The price at the shop is different

We show each shop's listed price when we last checked (prices are kept for up to 6 hours). Promotions change, and some shops show member-only or app-only prices. Tap an item to see what we found at every shop, then Check prices again for the latest.

It can't find my item, or picked the wrong thing

Use a simple name like “basmati rice” or “oat milk”. In Buy, every option is a card — tap the right one and we'll remember it next time. Quantities like “2x eggs” work too.

How do I put it on my iPhone's Home Screen?

Open saket-vanga.github.io/gotlobang in Safari, tap Share (or ••• then Share), then Add to Home Screen, keep Open as Web App on and tap Add.

My Mac says it can't open the app

It's not from the App Store, so the first time macOS asks you to confirm. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.

Are my lists private?

Yes. There's no account and your lists stay on your device. The app only talks to the shops' own websites to look up prices. Chats here are only seen by us.
